# Privacy Policy

APK Installer · Publisher: Rajmane · Last updated: October 09, 2026

> **In short:** APK Installer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no purchases. Your files stay on your device.

## 1. Who we are

APK Installer is an app for Fire TV devices and Fire tablets that installs Android app files (APK, XAPK, APKS and APKM) that you already have. It is published by Rajmane. You can contact us at [prathmesh.rajmane1@gmail.com](mailto:prathmesh.rajmane1@gmail.com).

## 2. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask you to sign in, does not create a profile, and does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We do not receive any information about you or your device.

## 3. Your files and apps

The app reads installer files on your device, such as files in the Downloads folder or on a USB drive, so it can show you what is inside and install the files you choose. It also reads the list of apps installed on the device so it can show whether an app is already installed and let you open, back up or uninstall apps you added from files. This information is used only on your device. It is not uploaded or sent anywhere.

## 4. Network use

The app does not browse the web and does not download anything from the internet. Its only network feature is a small page that the “From your phone” and “Transfer” screens open on your own Wi-Fi network, so that a phone or computer on the same network can send a file to this device or receive one from it.

- The page works only while that screen is open on your device.
- Each session needs a random short code that is shown on your device’s screen.
- Files travel only between your own devices on your own network. Nothing is sent to us or to any other server.

## 5. Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why it is needed |
|---|---|
| Install packages | To start the system installer for the files you choose. Android asks you to confirm every install. |
| Delete packages | To open the system screen that uninstalls an app you choose. |
| Storage and all files access | To find installer files in Downloads and on USB drives, and to save a backup copy of an app you added. |
| Query installed apps | To show whether an app is already installed and to list the apps you added from files. |
| Network | Only for the local Wi-Fi page described in section 4. |

The app never changes system settings by itself. When setup is needed, it opens the relevant Android settings page and you decide what to switch on.

## 6. Sharing

We do not share information with anyone, because we do not collect any. If you use the share button on an installer file, the file goes only to the app you pick in your device’s share menu.

## 7. Security

Files you receive over Wi-Fi are checked to be installer files before they are kept, and files that are not installers are discarded. The Wi-Fi page is protected by the random code described in section 4 and stops when you leave the screen.

## 8. Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## 9. Accounts and data deletion

There are no accounts, so there is no account data to delete. Backup copies and received files are stored on your device. You can delete them with any file manager, by clearing the app’s storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.
